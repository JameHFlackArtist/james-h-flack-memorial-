About Jim

The Rev. James Hall Flack — known to all as Jim — was a contemporary Irish watercolour painter. Born in County Armagh, he settled in Athy, County Kildare in the early 1970s, and the town and its surrounding countryside remained his home and his inspiration for the rest of his life.

Ordained to the Presbyterian ministry, Jim held the degrees of BA, BD, M.Th. and H.Dip.Ed. from Trinity College Dublin and Edinburgh University. He ministered at Athy, where his Service of Thanksgiving was held at Athy Presbyterian Church on 27 March 2018.

As an artist he was a member of the Water Colour Society of Ireland, the Ulster Watercolour Society, the Dublin Art Club and the Dublin Sketching Club. He exhibited widely in Ireland, Canada and the United States, and in 2000 received an Athy Urban District Council award for outstanding achievement in the arts.

His watercolours — quiet landscapes, woodlands, blossoms and skies rendered in soft, luminous washes — captured the gentle beauty of the Irish countryside he loved.
